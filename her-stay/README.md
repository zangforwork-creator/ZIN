# HER STAY

HerStay is a proposed travel booking platform for women. The concept would show accommodation safety criteria before booking, offer filters for women-only rooms or areas, and connect travellers to verified transfers and a planned support channel. A pilot in Da Nang is proposed.

Open `index.html` or visit `/ZIN/her-stay/` on GitHub Pages. This is a concept preview: there are no live listings, bookings, payments, partner verifications, or active account sign-in. The sign-in window does not send or save entered details.

Research figures on the page link to Agoda, Hostelworld, OECD, and the National Statistics Office of Vietnam. Those sources describe different surveys and the overall travel market; they are not demand estimates for HerStay.
