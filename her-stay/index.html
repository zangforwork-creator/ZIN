A few questions.

What is HER STAY?

HER STAY is a community concept for women who love travel, want to share experiences, and hope to make new friends along the way.

Can I join HER STAY now?

Not yet. This is an introduction to the idea. Membership signups will be added when the community is ready to launch.

Would I have to host or stay with someone?

No. You choose how you connect, whether that means chatting, meeting up, or sharing travel advice.
