About HerStay
✳

Stay choices with more clarity.

HerStay brings sample accommodation options and clear planning information into one journey for women travelling solo or with friends. Inspired by the convenience of platforms that combine travel services, the proposal puts visible safety information at the centre of each stay.

The concept draws on the multi-service model shown by Klook. HerStay is an independent proposal and is not affiliated with Klook.

Why explore this model?

The need is worth testing.

These figures describe different surveys and markets. They help shape the idea, but do not measure demand for HerStay itself.

15%

Solo travel interest

Of women surveyed across Asia by Agoda said they preferred travelling solo. The figures were 22% in Thailand and 25% in Hong Kong.

Agoda, 2025
55% vs 18%

Safety concerns

In Hostelworld's survey of 3,334 solo travellers, 55% of women and 18% of men said safety was a concern.

Hostelworld, 2025
13 points

Night-time safety gap

Across Asia-Pacific, women were on average 13 percentage points less likely than men to feel safe walking alone at night where they live; the gap was around 30 points in Malaysia.

OECD, 2025, Fig. 7.7

Hostelworld asked travellers about travel concerns; the OECD measure concerns perceived safety near home. They should not be read as the same measure.

Good to know

A few questions.

Can I reserve a real hotel room now?

You can search sample stays and save a request in this browser. No hotel receives or confirms it, and no payment is taken.

Does a listed criterion guarantee safety?

No single feature can guarantee safety. The proposal is to make each partner's conditions visible so travellers can make a more informed choice.

Is 24/7 support available today?

Not yet. A round-the-clock support channel is a planned feature to be tested as part of the pilot.

Is account sign-in active?

Google, email, and phone account sign-in requires an authentication service. You can use the stay planner without an account.
