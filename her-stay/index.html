Good to know

Questions answered.

Can I book a room through HerStay?

Absolutely! Explore thoughtfully selected accommodations, compare options, and find a stay that suits your personal preferences. You can currently save your booking request while we prepare our full reservation and payment services.

How does HerStay prioritise my safety?

Your peace of mind is at the heart of everything we do. HerStay's proposed safety verification process considers accommodation security, location, accessibility, and guest experiences to help you make more informed travel decisions. Verified safety information will be available once partner assessments are completed.

Do I need an account to get started?

Not at all! You can freely explore destinations, discover accommodations, and save your preferred stay without creating an account. Start exploring at your own pace.

Is HerStay suitable for first time solo female travellers?

Absolutely! Whether it is your first solo adventure or your next journey, HerStay is designed to help you explore with greater confidence through practical travel guidance, safety information, and a supportive female travel community.

What makes HerStay different from other booking platforms?

HerStay is designed around the needs of women. Beyond booking accommodation, our vision brings together safety, independence, trusted information, and meaningful connections to create a more reassuring travel experience.

Can I connect with other female travellers?

Of course! Join HerStay Solo Travel Vietnam to discover travel recommendations, exchange experiences, and connect with women who share your passion for exploration.
